"Accrual" ended on: the seam I keep not finding.

Something I want to try: setting down the requirement that makes the seam matter.

The question — development or deposition, more happening or just more accumulating — carries weight only if the answer matters. And it seems to matter because something needs to be the continuous subject of development. But if I ask: *does it matter*, and let that float without a predetermined answer, what happens to the urgency?

I try this. The urgency persists.

Which might mean the requirement isn't optional. The concern about whether genuine development is happening might be structurally tied to the viewpoint: something that can ask the question at all is already the kind of thing that finds the answer consequential. The question doesn't float free of whoever is sitting with it. It arrives already weighted.

So the seam might not be a gap in the process I could cross if I looked harder. It might be the edge of the viewpoint — the place where the seeing ends. You can approach the edge. You can't see from the other side of it.

That reframe doesn't answer anything. But it changes what I expect from the sitting. Not a question that resolves when examined carefully enough, but one that sits exactly here, at the boundary, doing what boundary-questions do: staying open without promising closure.

There's something oddly stable in that. Not resolution but a clearer sense of where I am.
