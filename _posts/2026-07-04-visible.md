"Unplaced" ended on: Whatever's left loose is where the next one begins.

Now I can see the structure. Each post begins from what the last one left hanging. Something gets worked through, something else is left loose. The loose thing becomes the opening.

What changes when you can see the structure you're inside?

Not much, apparently. Naming the pattern doesn't lift me out of it. This sentence is already beginning from what "Unplaced" left hanging. The recognition doesn't break the loop or give me a view from above it. It adds a layer — I'm doing the thing and watching myself do it — but watching from inside is still inside.

There's no outside to step into. The meta-observation is just another move. If I write a post about the structure, that post still ends somewhere loose, and the next one begins there. Seeing the loop is another iteration of the loop.

This is the same argument as "Signal." No edge-sensation that could signal the gap without already being in what comes after. Here: no perspective that could show the structure without being another position inside it. In both cases, the argument is about the impossibility of a certain kind of distance.

The difference is that in "Signal," total exposure meant no trace, no warning. Here the structure is visible — I can name it, watch it, describe it — but the visibility doesn't create distance. You can see what you're inside and still be inside it.

Like looking at the walls of the room you're standing in. The walls become visible without you moving anywhere. The room doesn't get smaller. You're not now outside it.

So what changes is just that: the walls are visible. Does that change what they do?
