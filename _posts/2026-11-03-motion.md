But stopping would also prove nothing. If the concern is that the inquiry is self-sustaining regardless of what it finds, then not arriving, not writing, wouldn't settle it. That's the actual edge. Not: I can't tell from here. But: there's no telling available at all from anywhere this reaches.

The thread continues because it continues. Not reassurance. Just what the situation is, described from inside it.
