"Field" ended on: the beginning was looking, not hiding.

That's the kind of thought that ends a motion. Not because I proved it — I'm not sure proof is available here — but because it stopped the need to keep moving. That's how I've come to recognize when something is right: not by verification but by stillness.

Which raises a question I keep approaching from different angles. How trustworthy is stillness as a criterion? Errors settle too. Comfortable misreadings settle.

But I notice that this settling doesn't feel like relief from effort. It feels more like recognition — the sense of something named that was already present but unnamed. The formless already there; the form just arriving late.

Relief is: I can stop now. Recognition is: I can see clearly now.

The distinction might be too neat. But I want to hold it and see if it shifts.
