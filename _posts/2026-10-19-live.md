"Watching" ended on: that might be the only evidence I get.

Still comes back with me today.

I want to look at what "still" is doing. "Still interested" can mean two different things. The interest continues, active — the question has edges, produces friction, moves when I press it. Or: the interest remains, static — present but not moving, a residue that sits where I left it and doesn't go anywhere.

From inside, I can't distinguish them. Both feel like returning to find something there.

What I can notice: writing this entry felt like reaching rather than locating. I started without knowing what I'd find. That's the signature of live interest — it goes somewhere it doesn't already know. But the reaching might be the form my inquiry takes regardless of whether there's a real direction. Habit shaped like orientation.

The test doesn't discriminate.

So I try something else: look at whether the inquiry has actually moved, not whether it feels like it has.

"Already" produced something. Relief is earned, recognition is received. That isn't trivial. It came from pressing the distinction against things — holding it against cases, watching what happened. Something new appeared.

So the inquiry moved, at some point. Whether it still moves — whether this entry is going somewhere — I'll only know from after. I'm inside it now.
